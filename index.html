How it works

Just drink. BottleMate does the rest.

Put your bottle on the base. This is what happens every time you take a sip.

  1. 1

    Lift your bottle

    The base notices and pauses the countdown.

  2. 2

    Drink, put it back

    Let go and wait about 3 seconds while it weighs.

  3. 3

    Sip logged

    A rising beep and a ripple on the light ring.

  4. 4

    Next sip re-planned

    A bigger drink buys a longer break before the next reminder.

BottleMate counts a sip only when the bottle was fully lifted for 1.5 s or more, is back and still for about 3 s, and the weight dropped by 25 ml or more. Bumps and a hand resting on it never count. Put back 30 g heavier and it's logged as a refill.

A goal made for you

Based on research, not a flat 2 litres.

Your daily target comes from the Yamada et al. 2022 (Science) water-turnover model: weight, sex, age, activity, and today's temperature, humidity and altitude. Water from food is subtracted (IOM 2005) and EFSA safety limits apply. A warm room or dry air brings the next reminder sooner.

  1. Tap Download APK on your phone.
  2. Open the file. If Android asks, allow Install unknown apps for your browser.
  3. Tap Install, then open BottleMate from the app drawer.

On a computer? Scan with your phone's camera to download.

Pair your base

  1. 1
    Plug in the base

    Any certified 5 V USB adapter (1 A or more). Place it on a flat, dry surface.

  2. 2
    Hold the knob for 3 s

    The ring shows a blue comet: pairing mode lasts 60 seconds.

  3. 3
    Add device in the app

    Tap Add device → Search for devices, keep the phone within 2 m.

  4. 4
    Calibrate in two steps

    Weigh the empty bottle, then the full one. That's it.

Troubleshooting

The screen is dark

It switched off to save power. Turn or press the knob: the first touch only wakes it. You can change the time in Menu › Display › Screen off.

A sip wasn't counted

Lift the bottle fully off the base for at least 1.5 s, put it back in the middle and let go. Drinks under 25 ml are ignored (Menu › Calibrate › Min sip).

The water level looks wrong

Using a new bottle? Run Menu › Calibrate › Full setup, or Device › Re-calibrate in the app.

The app can't find the base

Hold the knob for 3 s (pairing lasts 60 s). Keep the phone within 2 m, with Bluetooth on and the app allowed to use Nearby devices.

"Scale · check the wiring" or an amber ring

The scale isn't answering. Unplug the base for 10 s and plug it back in. If it stays, contact support.

Wrong time after a power cut

Open the app (it sets the clock), or use Menu › Clock › Set date/time on the base.
