The science

A goal shaped by you and your day.

Most apps say "drink 8 glasses". BottleMate uses the water-turnover model from Yamada et al., Science (2022): your weight, sex, age and activity, plus the temperature and humidity of your room right now. Water from food is subtracted and safety limits are applied.

A wellness aid, not a medical device. If you have heart, kidney or liver disease, or are on a fluid restriction, follow your doctor's advice.

Support

A little help getting set up.

How do I set up and calibrate the base?
Charge and switch it on: the ring sweeps aqua and the screen says Hello! Press the knob and follow 3 steps: empty base, then empty bottle, then full bottle.
Does it work with my bottle?
Yes: any bottle, flask, tumbler or mug that sits flat on the platform. You calibrate it once with the empty and full bottle.
Do I need the app or an account?
No. The base works fully on its own. The app adds a dashboard, history, the 3D body view, notifications and cloud backup. You can use the app as a guest.
How do I pair it with my phone?
In the app: Device → Add device → Search. On the base, hold the knob for 3 seconds until the ring shows a green comet. Keep the phone within 2 m.
Why didn't it count my sip?
A sip counts when the bottle was lifted for at least 1.5 s and came back at least 25 ml lighter. Put it back in the middle and hold still for a moment.
Will it nag me all day?
No. Every sip pushes the next reminder further away, there are quiet hours at night, a silent mode, and one press of the knob says "On it".
What happens to my data?
As a guest, everything stays on your phone. If you sign in, your data syncs to your own private cloud space and you can delete your account at any time.
Is there an iPhone app?
Not yet. The app is Android only for now; the base works without a phone.

The build

Designed and built by Group 05.

BottleMate began as a university embedded-systems project. An Arduino prototype grew into a standalone base with a hand-etched custom PCB, a 3D-designed enclosure, its own firmware, an Android app, a printed manual and packaging.
