How set-up works

Ready in five steps.

The app walks you through everything. Your profile sets the goal; pairing and calibration take about a minute.

  1. 1

    Sign in or skip

    Email, Google, or continue as a guest.

  2. 2

    About you

    Weight, age, activity, wake-up and bed time, climate.

  3. 3

    Your daily goal

    Calculated from the research model and explained.

  4. 4

    Pair the base

    Hold the knob 3 s (green comet), then tap Search.

  5. 5

    Calibrate

    Empty bottle, then full. Live weight on phone and base.

The app GUI

Five tabs along the bottom.

Greeting and base status, a liquid progress ring with today's ml and %, a pace hint, the weather strip (room and outside temperature, humidity), the Next sip card with the reason, Left to drink, live readings from the base and today's sips.

Two themes

Light or dark. Your call.

Both themes share the same glassy cards over calm water scenes. Switch any time in Profile; the status bar follows along.

Smart features

Quietly clever.

Adaptive reminders

Phone notifications that respect quiet hours, with haptics on sips and reminders.

Background tracking

A quiet service keeps the Bluetooth link alive when the app is closed. Sips and refills arrive as notifications.

Research-based goal

Yamada 2022 model, updated with today's temperature, humidity and altitude.

Offline history sync

The base logs up to 500 records while your phone is away; the app catches up when it reconnects.

Wi-Fi cloud backup

The base can upload to your cloud space by itself, even when no phone is near.

Your data, your choice

Guest mode keeps everything on the phone. Signed in, data lives in your own space; delete your account any time.
